Start 2027 with a clear plan and a simple way to stick to it.

This printable Habit & Goal Planner is a 13-page PDF set. Print only the pages you need, as often as you like. The trackers are undated, so they work every month and every year.

- My Top 3 Goals
- Goal Action Plan: milestones, obstacles, rewards
- Quarterly Goals (Q1 to Q4)
- Monthly Habit Tracker: 8 habits x 31 days, plus a mood column
- Weekly Habit Tracker: two weeks per page
- 2027 in Pixels: a 365-day tracker for mood, sleep or a habit
- 30-Day Challenge tracker
- Monthly Reflection
- Year-End Review

FILES

- Fits a standard binder or clipboard. You can also import it into a note-taking app that supports PDFs.
