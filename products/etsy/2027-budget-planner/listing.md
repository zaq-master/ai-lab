## Description

Plan every month of 2027 and see where your money goes.

This printable Budget Planner is a 14-page PDF set. Print only the pages you need, as often as you like. Most pages are undated, so they work every month. There are no currency symbols, so you can use any currency.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (14 pages)
- Cover with contents page
- 2027 Year at a Glance calendar (Sunday start) with a key for paydays and bill due dates
- Money Goals: 3 goals with target amount, target date and amount per month
- Annual Overview: income, expenses, savings, debt paid and money left over for each month
- Monthly Budget: income, savings, fixed and variable expenses, planned vs actual
- Paycheck Budget: two paychecks per page
- Bill Tracker: 18 bills x 12 months on one page
- Expense Tracker: 28 lines per page
- Weekly Spending Log: two weeks per page, with a need/want column
- Savings Goal Tracker: two goals, 40 circles each to colour in
- Sinking Funds: six funds per page
- Debt Payoff Tracker: list of debts plus a 24-line payment log and a 10% to 100% progress bar
- No-Spend Challenge: 30 days
- Monthly Money Review

FILES
- 2027-Budget-Planner_US-Letter.pdf (8.5 x 11 in)
- 2027-Budget-Planner_A4.pdf (210 x 297 mm)

DESIGN
- Minimal layout with soft lavender, blush and sage accents. Light enough to print in colour or black and white.
- 0.5 in margins, so it prints on home printers.
- Fits a standard binder or clipboard. You can also import it into a note-taking app that supports PDFs.
- Matches the DreamyPixelCo 2027 Habit & Goal Planner.

- Colours may look slightly different depending on your screen and printer.
